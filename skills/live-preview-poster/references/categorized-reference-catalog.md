## Cross-Category Rules

- Use 9:16: 1080x1920, 900x1600, 1242x2208, or 1440x2560.
- Preserve the uploaded person's original face pixels; do not regenerate the face.
- Separate workflow: original person cutout + original/generative background + typography + QR module.
- QR placeholder should be 14-20% canvas width and placed bottom right or bottom center.
- Title and live time should remain readable on mobile.
- Use no more than two primary font families and three dominant colors unless a specific campaign style requires more.
