## Common Patterns Observed

- Vertical mobile-first formats dominate: 9:16 or close variants such as 1080x1920, 900x1600, 1242x2208.
- Person portrait is the main conversion hook. Single-person posters often allocate roughly half to two-thirds of the canvas to the person.
- Clear information tiers work best: main title, live time, host/guest identity, short benefit line, CTA/QR.
- Many templates use upper/lower structure: portrait or hero visual in one large zone, title/time/CTA in the other.
- Entertainment and music posters favor bold modern sans-serif, high contrast, neon or saturated accents.
- Finance, education, and professional livestreams favor cleaner structure, blue/green/dark palettes, and authority-focused host presentation.
- Commerce posters use hotter colors, big promotional labels, gift/discount shapes, and more urgent CTAs.
- QR area should sit in a calm container, usually bottom right or bottom center, and should not compete with the face.

## Five Style Directions For This Skill

### 1. High-Energy Commerce

Use for sales livestreams, coupons, new product launches, festival campaigns.

- Color: red, pink, orange, yellow accents, with white or dark contrast blocks.
- Layout: large person cutout lower center or lower right; big title upper left; benefits and time in stacked labels.
- Details: diagonal shapes, burst tags, glow outlines, gift or coupon motifs.
- QR: bottom right in a white rounded-square or simple white card.
- Avoid: crowding the face with discount badges.

### 2. Premium Magazine

Use for beauty, fashion, personal brand, expert host, luxury/service events.

- Color: black, charcoal, ivory, champagne gold, muted neutrals.
- Layout: full-height portrait on one side; sparse editorial text on the other; strong negative space.
- Details: subtle grain, soft spotlight, thin divider lines, serif or refined sans pairing.
- QR: small but readable, bottom corner with minimal label.
- Avoid: overdecorating; premium depends on restraint.

### 3. Tech Neon Stage

Use for AI, digital products, tech talks, game/e-sports, futuristic launches.

- Color: dark background with cyan, blue, magenta, violet highlights.
- Layout: person center or right, with virtual stage panels behind; title in strong block type.
- Details: grid, glass panels, light beams, waveform or data-line motifs.
- QR: bottom right in a high-contrast glass/white module.
- Avoid: placing neon highlights across eyes, nose, or mouth.

### 4. Warm Lifestyle

Use for education, coaching, wellness, community, creator updates, softer personal branding.

- Color: warm cream, peach, soft green, light orange, gentle pink.
- Layout: friendly bust/half-body portrait lower center; title in clean warm type; schedule block near mid/lower area.
- Details: soft shadows, paper texture, simple stickers, round badges, approachable CTA.
- QR: bottom center or bottom right with generous breathing room.
- Avoid: beige-only palettes; keep at least one crisp accent color for hierarchy.

### 5. Minimal Platform Card

Use for recurring shows, knowledge streams, business updates, professional announcements.

- Color: white/black/gray base with one brand accent.
- Layout: app-like grid; person image in a fixed frame or large cutout; schedule block and QR aligned to grid.
- Details: pill labels, calendar/time module, clean dividers, compact iconography.
- QR: precise card module, same grid as schedule info.
- Avoid: making it look like a landing page; it should remain a poster with strong first-glance hierarchy.

## Generation/Composition Rules

- Use the uploaded person photo as the protected foreground layer.
- Segment the person and preserve original face pixels; do not regenerate or redraw the face.
- Create backgrounds, typography, decorative shapes, and QR placeholder separately.
- Apply only low-risk global harmonization to the person: color balance, exposure, edge cleanup, shadow/rim light.
- Keep all typography and graphics outside the facial region.
- Preserve both goals: recognizable original teacher/IP face and a polished poster style. Achieve style through the surrounding design system, not facial alteration.
- When exact livestream copy is absent, use placeholders: `直播预告`, `今晚 20:00`, `扫码预约`, `主题待定`.
